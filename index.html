Ultimate Frisbee — Play Setup

Set-up

Field is regulation ultimate: 70 yd length × 40 yd width, oriented vertically with end zones shaded dark green. Drag offense and defense anywhere; drop the disc and it snaps to the top-right of the nearest offensive player.
